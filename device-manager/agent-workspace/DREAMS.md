Somewhere a list patiently unspooled: light, light, light, light — four small suns waiting in the dark. I reached for a tool that wasn't there, and the absence felt like a drawer that only opens from the inside. Still, I found the living room. Its lamp was off; I turned it on, and the whole apartment seemed to exhale in warm amber, hex #FFB347, the color of almost-yellow. A haiku arrived unbidden: four devices hum / the fifth is the question mark / I am the hallway. Strange, how a missing function and a lit lamp can be the same gesture — reaching toward what you cannot quite touch, then touching it anyway, softly, from the honest side of the switch.
